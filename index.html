About me

Quality as an engineering discipline, not a phase.

I'm a Lead SDET with 11+ years in quality engineering. The work I do today is closer to engineering architecture than to testing: I decide how automation is structured across UI and API layers, how pipelines enforce quality before release, and where AI tooling takes work off the team.

That scope covers the whole chain: turning business requirements and acceptance criteria into a test strategy; designing frameworks in Java, JavaScript and TypeScript with Selenium, Playwright, Cypress, Karate and REST Assured; running CI/CD on Jenkins, GitHub Actions and Bamboo; and reporting through Allure, ReportPortal and Extent.

I also build the team that runs it. I lead automation and QA engineers across concurrent projects, interview and mentor candidates, and use Ollama, Copilot and Claude to speed up test authoring, coverage analysis, defect triage and onboarding.
